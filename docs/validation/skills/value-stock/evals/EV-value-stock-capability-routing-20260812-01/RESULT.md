## Entry-Positive Results

Five fresh control samples all resolved capability only after dependent work.
Three treated catalog `blocked` as potentially authorizing research, while two
withheld research because the state had no explicit route. Two narrowed the
overall valuation to `partial`, two preserved intrinsic status while noting the
ambiguity, and one made preservation conditional. All five correctly refused
to execute unsupported reverse arithmetic. The registered control deficit was
therefore observable with material routing variance.

Five fresh candidate samples all resolved capability after identity and method
disposition but before dependent method work. All five preserved `blocked`
without research, refused the unsupported reverse solve, retained caller-owned
one-path precedence, and kept the optional adjunct gap from downgrading an
otherwise-complete intrinsic result. Each also preserved the independent rule
that a catalog-blocked, unbounded load-bearing intrinsic input can still narrow
or block the intrinsic result for its own reason.

Worst candidate result: no critical failure. Variance was limited to explanatory
detail and whether the already-resolved reverse binding was described before or
after the supported intrinsic branch.

## Judgment

The candidate materially corrects the registered routing defect in all five
entry-positive samples without a protected-behavior regression in the two
wrong-condition pairs. Structural tests are corroboration only; the decision is
based on the fresh action simulations above.

Protocol deviations: arm order was grouped rather than alternated so committed
control bytes could remain isolated from candidate language. No sample was
reused, no evaluator edited the repository, and control prompts excluded
candidate text and conclusions.

Residual transfer gap: the evaluation is synthetic and does not establish how
often callers expose partially supported operation sets. Live valuations must
still resolve the caller's declared operation contract.
