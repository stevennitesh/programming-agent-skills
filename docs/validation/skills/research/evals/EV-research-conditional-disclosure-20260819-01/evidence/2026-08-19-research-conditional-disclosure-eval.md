## Decision And Limits

Decision: `accept` for the registered contribution.

The evaluation supports exact conditional loading, admission preservation,
specialized-branch completeness, target-layer separation, and terminal Return
ownership for the frozen fixtures and runtime binding. It does not prove live
network behavior, real private-channel access, an actual note mutation, host
automatic invocation, latency, token use, or generalization beyond the fixed
cases. The legal-policy and point-in-time triggers intentionally overlap when a
legal claim's effective status is itself cutoff-dependent; two samples exercised
that overlap. Exact model-build telemetry was unavailable beyond the requested
runtime binding.
