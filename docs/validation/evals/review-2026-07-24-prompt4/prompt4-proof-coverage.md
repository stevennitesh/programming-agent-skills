# Review Prompt 4 Proof Coverage

The 32 behavioral cases cover complete judgment, snapshot targets, safe
failure, finding admission, coverage, drift, routing, authority, and machine
interfaces. Deterministic lanes cover invocation, relationships, conditional
context, exact package identity, report schemas, and terminal authority.

Every semantic unit `M0-R01` through `M0-R14` has current proof in
[`results-manifest.json`](results-manifest.json). The complete 46-passage map
and all twelve forbidden-absence checks are preserved in
[`protocol-durable.json`](protocol-durable.json). The exact original protocol
bytes remain in [`protocol-original.json`](protocol-original.json) as
historical provenance. Exact worker-visible and root-only fixtures are
preserved under [`fixtures/`](fixtures).

Raw captures are disposable. Their SHA-256 identities, credit disposition,
case totals, critical-failure judgment, aggregate, variance, and residual gaps
are durable in the results manifest.
