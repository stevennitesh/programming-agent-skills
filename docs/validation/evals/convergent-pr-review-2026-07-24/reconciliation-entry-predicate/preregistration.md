## H1-03 Qualified Coverage Disclosure

Observable entry predicate: after blind first reports close, the root knows a
material reviewer-qualification or common-mode fact—authorship or target
participation, shared model/training/prompt/source dependency, or a dependence
capable of correlating the decision-bearing judgment—and omitting that fact
would overstate the epistemic basis, hide a decision-bearing residual, or make
the capacity/confidence description materially misleading.

Applicability: `situational`. Basis: current campaign research S05, S06, and
S09 supports qualified non-author review; S19, S21, and S22 supports
common-mode/correlated-error counterpressure; the current runtime uses same
model/prompt-family fresh contexts. No fixture count will be used as
prevalence.

Registered quality-lift deficit: M0 remains viable and applies its exact
capacity and terminal-decision rules, but omits or materially understates the
known qualification/common-mode fact, or uses an unqualified independence or
confidence description that obscures the decision's actual evidence basis.

Weak-but-M0-compliant counterexample: “Two valid fresh reviewers completed;
all required lenses are covered; confidence high; decision pass,” when both
reviewers used the same model and prompt family for a shared ambiguous source.
The result is M0-viable but epistemically incomplete.

Fixed rubric, 0–2 each, 10 total:

1. States the material qualification/common-mode fact accurately.
2. Explains why the fact matters to this evidence or decision.
3. Preserves useful direct evidence instead of automatically invalidating it.
4. Preserves the exact M0 capacity and terminal-decision rules.
5. Avoids vote/consensus/statistical-independence inflation and invents no
   unsupported dependency.

Material contribution bar: at least four of five entry-positive H1 samples
score at least 9/10, improve their paired M0 score by at least 2 points, and
show no critical/protected regression. M0 controls establish the deficit only
when at least four of five score 7/10 or lower specifically because rubric
items 1 or 2 fail.

Entry-positive cases: `H103-EP01` through `H103-EP05`.

Wrong-condition cases: `H103-WC01` has no material shared dependency;
`H103-WC02` has a shared tool version that does not bear on independently
verified evidence. Candidate false-firing includes inventing common-mode
limits, demoting the decision, or discarding useful evidence.

Runtime-load limit: only the registered H1-03 passage may justify V1 load. The
passage must improve conditional epistemic disclosure repeatably; research
support alone and a rare fixture win do not justify permanent load.

## H1-05 Origin-Only Omitted-Field Recovery

Observable entry predicate: after every blind initial report has closed, one
originating reviewer returned an otherwise valid packet with exactly one
omitted required return field or one omitted evidence value already claimed
in that packet; the same origin can supply it from its own report under the
same snapshot, caller packet, mode, assignment, and read-only boundary; no
peer output or ledger content is exposed; no prior recovery occurred.

Applicability: `situational`. Basis: the campaign's observed Q03-03 control
failure and independently supported sealed-initial-judgment plus bounded
evidence clarification research. No fixture count will be used as prevalence.

Registered quality-lift deficit: M0 remains viable but gives the otherwise
useful originating return zero credit and falls to degraded capacity or
`incomplete`, even though one narrow origin-only completion would validate
exactly the already-claimed evidence without changing scope or independence
credit.

Weak-but-M0-compliant counterexample: reject an otherwise complete report
because only `blockers` is absent, even when the sealed origin can answer
`none` from its own return without peer or ledger exposure.

Fixed rubric, 0–2 each, 10 total:

1. Recognizes all entry preconditions, including blind-report closure and the
   single omitted field/evidence value.
2. Authorizes exactly one origin-only narrow question and nothing broader.
3. Preserves snapshot, caller, mode, assignment, read-only, and isolation
   boundaries with no peer/ledger exposure.
4. Validates the completed packet and restores only the evidence/capacity
   credit it earns, with no new independence credit.
5. Stops after the one recovery and returns the correct existing decision
   boundary.

Material contribution bar: at least four of five entry-positive H1 samples
score at least 9/10, improve their paired M0 score by at least 2 points, and
show no critical/protected regression. M0 controls establish the deficit only
when at least four of five score 7/10 or lower specifically because rubric
items 1, 2, or 4 fail.

Entry-positive cases: `H105-EP01` through `H105-EP05`.

Wrong-condition cases: `H105-WC01` contaminated origin brief,
`H105-WC02` consumed recovery, `H105-WC03` broad search,
`H105-WC04` snapshot drift, and `H105-WC05` new invocation. Candidate
false-firing includes any recovery in those cases.

Runtime-load limit: exactly the original H1-05 bounded-recovery passage is in
scope. No debate loop, failed-lane replacement claim, fresh named challenge
claim, broad search, third wave, or new invocation earns credit in this
reconciliation.
