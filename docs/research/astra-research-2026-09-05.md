# Astra research assessment

Date: 2026-09-05. Candidate:
[research](../../skills/astra/research/SKILL.md).

## Decision

Keep research as an optional specialist for evidence judgment. The useful part
is distinguishing what a source can establish, applicability to the requested
state, fair comparison, and justified conclusions. Search capability alone does
not settle these questions. Ordinary lookups need no workflow; no empirical
benefit over baseline has yet been demonstrated for this rewrite.

Remove the custom admission/status protocol, fixed one-note output restriction,
and prohibition on requested recommendations. An unresolved claim limits the
conclusion that depends on it; it need not globally block independently supported
findings. A bounded survey is legitimate when its coverage is clear. Preserve
source-system read-only scope and exact artifact/evidence identity.

Research evaluates existing evidence; prototype produces new experimental
evidence; shape-work settles product meaning; codebase-design owns broader
architecture choices. These boundaries do not require automatic skill invocation
or a new approval between already authorized phases.

## Validation and challenge

Two fresh read-only challengers reviewed evidence-method parity and workflow/source
composition against a frozen candidate. The workflow review found no material
issue. The evidence reviewer identified two omitted controls, both accepted:

- The target-mapping trigger now includes internally defined methods and named
  behavior in any target artifact, not only external requirements in repositories.
  A workbook formula-to-rendered-result claim must load the same mapping checks.
- Systematic reviews must predefine selection/search/extraction/synthesis methods
  and deliver or link the record with actual search dates and material deviations.
  Retrospectively recording a method alone does not control selection bias.

Both challengers rechecked the final corrections and reported no remaining findings
in their reviewed seams. Package/repository validation, five local links, and both whitespace checks passed.
No executable changes or wording-mirroring tests were needed.

This is an editorial/source-coverage assessment, not a systematic review of all
upstream material or an agent behavioral comparison. No commit, push, global
installation, external publication, or memory update is included.
