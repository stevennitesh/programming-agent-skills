# Astra change-review rewrite

This records source selection and review of the Astra candidate. It is not proof
that the skill improves review quality over a capable model and repository contract.

## Composition

One change-review skill owns ordinary review and an explicitly user-selected
high-assurance branch. Ordinary review is read-only and does not delegate. A PR,
large change, or request for a final review does not automatically activate high
assurance. Repair and external publication remain separate effects governed by
the user's request. A review-and-fix request retains its authorization across the
review phase.

The main procedure selects the actual candidate, traces intended behavior and
engineering quality separately, challenges findings with evidence, and reports
findings and coverage limits. Finding admission, gate decisions, and remediation
share one reference. The optional reference owns fresh reviewer dispatch and
evidence-based convergence instead of duplicating the review method.

## Source selection

Reviewed custom change-review, its finding and formal-review contracts, and the
complete custom high-assurance-review procedure. Retain fixed candidate/context
identity, reachable findings, non-call-graph consumers, actual producer-to-consumer
proof, required versus optional evidence, stable remediation IDs, independence,
and blocker/coverage distinctions. Remove fixed admission packets, mandatory
specification setup, and failure merely because a selected diff is empty.

High assurance preserves two independent perspectives on the same candidate,
with overlapping obligations and different emphasis. Initial briefs omit author
hypotheses and peer output. The coordinator verifies material claims, resolves
disputes without voting, and reports incomplete coverage even when a blocker
already decides the gate. Snapshot or reviewer limitations cannot be relabeled
as completed high assurance. A coordinator who authored the change must disclose
that role instead of claiming independence for the synthesis.

## Challenger review

Two fresh-context reviewers examined the fixed candidate read-only. One compared
quality and evidence details with custom and all four upstream sources; the other
checked selection, authority, optional orchestration, drift, and remediation.
Both passed their assigned scopes without actionable findings.

Their scenarios included staged-only review, exact comparisons, absent intent,
moving context, an author-coordinator, unavailable independent reviewers,
producer-only proof hiding a stale consumer, justified compatibility, concrete
maintainability costs, unchanged-line regressions, and missing remediation history.
The skill validator, repository validator, local link checks, and whitespace
checks passed. No review, comment publication, repair, or installation was invoked
by authoring this package.

## Verification limits

These are instruction-only changes. Package and link checks verify structure;
challengers check semantic omissions and workflow contradictions. Actual review
effectiveness, false-positive rates, and missed regressions need separate
behavioral evaluation. Custom sources and consumers remain unchanged; source
creation does not install the Astra package or migrate legacy review workflows.
