# Research Catalog And Research Card Retrieval Contract

Status: answered

Applicable state: repository worktree at
`a124384d2cc9e82d5be29db2359783acba6703ee`, inspected 2026-07-25
America/Chicago. External standards were checked in their versions identified

Return owner: Wayfinder map
[Chart the Fresh Composition Epoch](https://github.com/stevennitesh/programming-agent-skills/issues/31).

## Question

Which evidence-card fields, classification boundaries, aliases, freshness
signals, retrieval dimensions, and stopping rules best support bounded
discovery of better steering or adjacent behavior without letting the catalog
anchor the independent pass or choose H1?

## Caller Use And Boundary

This note supplies evidence and a recommended retrieval contract for the Fresh
Composition Epoch. It may constrain the later topology, prototype, Pack
Composition Contract, and Deploy Campaign decisions.

It does not:

- choose a skill inventory or composition;
- admit, reject, rank, or prove H1 behavior;
- replace the independent problem-first pass;
- prescribe exact Markdown, YAML, JSON, RDF, or database serialization;
- migrate historical artifacts; or
- authorize runtime, campaign, installation, or Git changes.

## Answer

Use a claim-addressable Research Card with stable identity, controlled lexical
labels, explicit applicability and counterconditions, claim-owning provenance,
derived freshness, and typed relationships. Generate a thin Research Catalog
index from those cards.

Open the catalog only after the independent problem-first packet has been
recorded and fingerprinted. Retrieve by failure and recruited behavior before
skill name or known solution. Return a caller-bounded set of canonical card
families grouped by transparent match class and ordered deterministically by
stable ID. If the eligible set exceeds the caller's bound, return an overflow
that asks for a narrower problem dimension rather than silently ranking or
truncating results.

The catalog returns evidence, counterevidence, conditions, limits, and source
pointers. A separate reconciliation record may classify what was retrieved,
but the card and catalog must contain no adoption, recommendation, H1
admission, or behavioral-efficacy field.

This shape is supported by:

- the repository's accepted independent-pass and historical-by-default
  constraints in [ADR-0009](../../adr/0009-fresh-composition-epochs-revalidate-skill-pack-knowledge.md);
- the current Deploy Research Pass separation of source claims, method
  evidence, applicability, counterpressure, freshness, and H1 consequences in
  [`deploy-prompts.md`](../../synthesis/methods/deploy-prompts.md);
- W3C DCAT's use of identifiers, descriptions, themes, relations, issued and
  modified dates, versioning, and conformance metadata for cataloged resources
  ([DCAT 3, W3C Recommendation, 2024](https://www.w3.org/TR/vocab-dcat-3/));
- W3C SKOS's disjoint preferred, alternative, and hidden labels plus typed
  semantic relations and scope notes
  ([SKOS Reference, W3C Recommendation, 2009](https://www.w3.org/TR/skos-reference/));
- W3C PROV-O's explicit derivation, revision, primary-source, generation, and
  invalidation relations
  ([PROV-O, W3C Recommendation, 2013](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/));
- DCMI's identifier, source, relation, provenance, issued, modified, valid,
  version, and replacement vocabulary
  ([DCMI Metadata Terms, 2020 recommendation](https://www.dublincore.org/specifications/dublin-core/dcmi-terms/));
- the FAIR principles' persistent-identifier, rich-metadata, and provenance
  pressure
  ([Wilkinson et al., 2016](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2016.18));
- PRISMA-S's requirement to report sources, exact strategies, limits, search
  dates, and deduplication
  ([Rethlefsen et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13643-020-01542-z)); and
- Cochrane's guidance to document the reason for stopping and use saturation
  or lack of new relevant retrieval rather than an arbitrary source quota
  ([Cochrane Handbook 6.5.1, chapter 4](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-04)).

These external vocabularies support the metadata and retrieval semantics. They
do not establish that RDF, a particular schema syntax, a candidate count, or
the exact local field names improve agent behavior.

## Minimum Research Card Contract

One card owns one reusable behavior or method under one coherent applicability
boundary. A card may contain multiple evidence claims, but each claim is
independently addressable.

| Group | Required Field | Contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Schema | `schema_version` | Version of the card contract, separate from the evidence revision. |
| Identity | `card_id` | Stable opaque identity; never derived solely from title or path and never reused. |
| Identity | `preferred_label` | One canonical human label. Renaming it does not change `card_id`. |
| Identity | `alternative_labels` | Genuine synonyms and legacy names used only for retrieval expansion. |
| Identity | `hidden_labels` | Misspellings, deprecated wording, or source-local terms useful for search but not display. |
| Lifecycle | `record_state` | `verified`, `superseded`, or `retired`; drafts are not catalog-visible. This is evidence-record state, not epoch admission. |
| Essence | `behavior` | Neutral statement of the method or behavior, not a recommendation. |
| Essence | `failure_prevented` | Concrete failure or weakness the behavior may address. |
| Essence | `recruited_behavior` | Action, judgment, gate, sequence, Return, or completion behavior the method is expected to recruit. |
| Scope | `scope_note` | Meaning boundary and explicit exclusions. |
| Scope | `applicability_conditions` | Conditions under which the evidence is relevant. |
| Scope | `counterconditions` | Conditions under which the method should not be inferred to apply. |
| Scope | `wrong_condition` | Representative condition in which importing the behavior would be unnecessary or harmful. |
| Evidence | `method_evidence` | Exactly one of `independently-supported`, `contested`, `pack-specific`, or `unverified`. |
| Evidence | `claims` | Claim records with stable `claim_id`, exact statement, `supported` / `conflicted` / `unknown` status, source IDs, counterevidence, applicability, and limits. |
| Provenance | `sources` | Source records with stable `source_id`, authority class, locator, exact revision/date/version/jurisdiction/population/method, inspected anchors, access date and depth, and source limits. |
| Freshness | `verified_at` | When the claims and source identities were last checked. |
| Freshness | `refresh_triggers` | Observable changes requiring recheck, such as a new governing version, changed revision, correction, retraction, access failure, applicability change, or material counterevidence. |
| Relations | `relations` | Typed card links: `broader`, `narrower`, `related`, `exact-match`, `close-match`, `conflicts-with`, `revision-of`, or `supersedes`. |
| Retrieval | `dimensions` | Controlled problem, behavior, condition, role, lifecycle-stage, source-class, and relationship tags. |
| Limits | `unknowns` | Material open evidence or applicability gaps. |
| Limits | `claim_limits` | What the evidence does not establish, including behavioral efficacy and transfer limits. |

Mechanically derive, rather than manually author, the card path, content
fingerprint, outgoing/incoming relationship checks, and catalog membership.
An H1 decision should cite exact `claim_id` values and the card fingerprint,
not only `card_id` or a broad packet.

## Catalog Record

The generated or mechanically validated catalog needs only:

- catalog schema and taxonomy versions;
- catalog fixed point and generated-at time;
- one entry per visible canonical card containing `card_id`, path, fingerprint,
  preferred and search labels, retrieval dimensions, method-evidence class,
  derived freshness state, and typed relations; and
- integrity results for unique IDs, resolvable paths, valid relation targets,
  claim/source links, and canonical-equivalence ownership.

The catalog must not copy claim narratives or citations from cards. It must not
contain recommendation rank, popularity, confidence score, adoption state, H1
decision, or validation result.

## Classification Boundaries

Keep these axes separate:

| Axis | Boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Source claim status | `supported`, `conflicted`, or `unknown` answers whether an inspected owner entails one exact claim. |
| Method evidence | `independently-supported`, `contested`, `pack-specific`, or `unverified` answers how broadly the proposed method is supported under recorded conditions. |
| Applicability | Conditions and counterconditions answer whether that evidence bears on the current problem; support does not imply applicability. |
| Retrieval match | `exact`, `close`, or `related` answers why a card was returned; match strength is not an adoption judgment. |
| Freshness | `verified-for-fixed-point`, `refresh-required`, or `unknown` is derived from identities, `verified_at`, policy, and triggers; freshness does not prove validity or efficacy. |
| Record lifecycle | `verified`, `superseded`, or `retired` describes the card record; it does not carry an epoch disposition. |
| Epoch disposition | `exact-reusable`, `method-evidence-only`, `historical-admission-only`, `superseded`, `unverifiable`, or `duplicate` belongs to the Fresh Composition Epoch's reconciliation, not the card. |
| H1 decision | Admission, rejection, local experiment, proof design, and behavioral result belong to per-skill synthesis and validation, never research metadata. |

A repeated label is not a duplicate. Treat two records as one canonical card
only when they describe the same essential behavior, applicability boundary,
and claim meaning. Merge their aliases and source pointers while preserving
provenance. Use `exact-match` for independently maintained equivalent cards,
`close-match` when scope or conditions differ, `conflicts-with` for
incompatible methods under overlapping conditions, and `supersedes` only when
a later evidence record intentionally replaces an earlier one.

## Alias Contract

Borrow the SKOS distinction without requiring RDF:

- `preferred_label`: one displayed canonical term;
- `alternative_labels`: true synonyms and renamed terms;
- `hidden_labels`: misspellings, deprecated phrases, and upstream-local wording
  used only to expand text search.

The three sets are disjoint. An alias resolves to one `card_id`; it never
creates semantic equivalence between cards. Colliding aliases produce an
explicit ambiguous result with candidate card IDs and scope notes rather than
an automatic choice.

## Freshness Contract

Do not store a bare `fresh: true`.

Freshness is a query-time result over:

1. the exact source and claim applicability identity;
2. `verified_at`;
3. source-specific refresh policy and observable triggers;
4. corrections, retractions, revisions, or new counterevidence; and
5. the intended application being queried.

Use `verified-for-fixed-point` only when all load-bearing claims still match
their fixed sources and the current application. Use `refresh-required` when a
trigger fired. Use `unknown` when identity, access, policy, or applicability
cannot be checked. A stable historical source can remain usable without a
recent publication date; a recently modified source can still be inapplicable.

## Retrieval Contract

### Admission

Refuse catalog retrieval with `catalog-not-admitted` unless the caller provides
the fingerprint of a recorded independent problem-first packet containing:

- intended essence and M0 units;
- named failures or expected weaknesses;
- discovered methods and alternatives;
- counterpressure and wrong conditions; and
- unresolved named gaps.

This proves ordering, not search quality. The catalog remains closed during
the independent pass.

### Query

The caller locks:

- one failure, weakness, or recruited behavior;
- applicable conditions and explicit exclusions;
- relevant role or relationship only when behaviorally material;
- accepted evidence and freshness filters;
- a maximum number of canonical card families; and
- the independent-packet fingerprint.

Query in this order:

1. failure or expected M0 weakness;
2. recruited behavior;
3. applicability and counterconditions;
4. owner, caller/callee role, and lifecycle stage;
5. method-evidence and freshness class; then
6. skill name, source family, or known method.

Apply aliases only after the problem query is fixed. Group results as `exact`,
`close`, and `related`; order within a group by stable `card_id`. Do not use
popularity, source count, prestige, incumbent presence, or an opaque relevance
score.

If eligible canonical families exceed the locked maximum, return
`catalog-overflow` with the count and available narrowing dimensions. Do not
silently return a top subset. The exact default maximum is an implementation
and prototype question, not supported by the inspected sources.

### Result

For each returned card provide:

- match class and the fields that matched;
- preferred label and `card_id`;
- behavior, failure, applicability, and counterconditions;
- method-evidence class;
- exact claim IDs and source pointers;
- freshness result, counterevidence, unknowns, and limits; and
- related, conflicting, superseding, or duplicate-candidate links.

A query-session disposition may record `relevant-evidence`,
`already-represented`, `not-applicable`, `counterevidence`, or
`unverified-gap`. These are reconciliation facts, not mutations to the card
and not H1 decisions.

## Stopping Rules

Use the already accepted epoch bound:

```text
one recorded problem-first independent pass
-> one catalog reconciliation pass
-> at most one named-gap pass
-> Prompt 2 reconciliation
```

Within catalog reconciliation:

1. inspect at most one canonical card per exact-equivalence family;
2. follow a card's source only when a load-bearing claim, freshness trigger,
   conflict, or applicability gap cannot be judged from the verified card;
3. classify every returned family and every load-bearing claim;
4. stop when another eligible family repeats the same behavior and conditions
   or cannot change the evidence classification, applicability boundary, or
   named gap;
5. return `catalog-overflow` instead of browsing beyond the caller's bound; and
6. permit one additional query only after naming the unresolved fact and how
   it could materially change H1 admission.

If the named gap remains unresolved, return it as an evidence gap. Do not
convert uncertainty into support and do not start a general catalog survey.
This is decision saturation, not a source quota or a claim of exhaustive
knowledge.

## Material Limits

- This is a design-research answer, not behavioral validation.
- The external standards target catalogs, provenance, controlled vocabularies,
  or systematic-review reporting; applying them to skill research is an
  explicit local inference.
- The note does not prove that a catalog reduces anchoring. The accepted ADR
  controls anchoring by ordering and sequestering the catalog.
- Exact schema syntax, candidate-bound defaults, indexing implementation,
  query ergonomics, and latency remain unproved.
- Existing historical artifacts have not been converted into cards or judged
  reusable.

## Stopping Basis

The governing local constraints, catalog metadata, alias relations,
provenance, search reporting, freshness, and stopping claims each have an
inspected primary or governing owner. Additional standards repeated the same
metadata families and could not settle the named unknowns of serialization,
candidate count, or behavioral effect. Those unknowns belong to the already
charted prototype and later design tickets.
