# Point-In-Time Evidence

Load this branch when a claim depends on what was available, known, published,
or effective as of a cutoff. Otherwise do not load it.

Define the cutoff and relevant availability channel. Distinguish the subject
date, the earliest availability established through an inspected channel, and
later revisions. A current page does not establish prior availability, and
publication does not prove that a decision-maker possessed the information.
Keep the claim `unknown` when the required historical channel or vintage cannot
be established.
