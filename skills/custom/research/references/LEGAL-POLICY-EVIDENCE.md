# Legal And Policy Evidence

Load this branch when a claim asserts legal or policy meaning, obligation,
permission, prohibition, or effective status in a jurisdiction and period.
Otherwise do not load it.

Define the jurisdiction and effective period. Distinguish operative text,
controlling interpretation, official guidance, observed practice, and
nonbinding or persuasive authority. Treat each source as authoritative only
for the legal or policy layer it owns, and preserve conflicts or unavailable
controlling authority under the common claim-status and Return rules.
