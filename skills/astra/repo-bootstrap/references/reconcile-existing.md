# Reconcile existing guidance

Use for requested setup or pack migrations. Ongoing maintenance of stale or
scattered guidance belongs to [context-hygiene](../../context-hygiene/SKILL.md).
Local wording differences from a current template do not require migration.

## Meaning to preserve

Distinguish deliberate local policy from inherited defaults. A rule copied by an
older bootstrap is not automatically an intentional override; equally, a new seed
does not justify replacing a real project choice. Use decisions, history, and
actual consumers when that distinction matters. Ask the owner only when unresolved
provenance would change a consequential policy decision.

Preserve verified repository facts, accepted domain meaning, useful rationale,
and deliberate exceptions unless reconsidering them is part of the request.
Historical records remain evidence rather than current instructions.

## Migration result

Follow the affected root and nested instructions, referenced guides, and narrow
mechanical checks far enough to reconcile changed behavior. Typical targets are
retired skill routes, obsolete mandatory workflows, stale setup fields or
commands, and conflicting owners.

Replace displaced rules at their current owners. If a retired workflow has no
replacement, permit direct work or remove its route; do not invent another
mandatory step. Equivalent local wording can stay. Adapt any check that encodes
a displaced convention while preserving its still-valid protection.

Apply settled changes within the authorized migration without another approval
cycle. Compare templates only where they clarify an affected inherited behavior;
the goal is coherent current guidance rather than template parity.

## Evidence and completion

Check that future agents reach current owners, no longer receive the displaced
rules, and can identify preserved local exceptions. Include affected consumers
and mechanical checks in that assessment. A passing legacy validator establishes
only its own rules, not current compatibility.

A repeat migration with no material behavioral difference should produce no edit.
Report migrated surfaces and any unresolved policy or access gap.
