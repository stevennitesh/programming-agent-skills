# Work-item brief

Use when writing an assignment or assessing readiness. This reference also serves
execution callers; reading it selects no workflow and grants no extra authority.
Repository ticket format and readiness conventions remain binding.

## Give the next actor enough context

Reuse an adequate ticket or linked source; repair only gaps or stale context.
Include what applies to the bounded outcome:

- intended recipient, purpose, scope, exclusions, and observable acceptance;
- current behavior or inspected evidence, constraints, and preservation needs;
- accessible source sections, code owners, interfaces, and decision rationale;
- required predecessor results, permissions, and consequential open gates; and
- binding choices distinguished from proposals and implementation freedom.

Carry distinguishing inputs, states, results, metrics, thresholds, baselines, and
operating conditions when generic wording could permit the wrong implementation.
Keep the source's decision authority. Identify its accepted revision when changes
could alter the assignment. Capture decisive conversation-only facts in the ticket
or an accessible shared source. A broad parent link does not replace local purpose,
acceptance, and precise source sections needed without the original chat.

Describe behavior and stable interfaces rather than speculative file choreography.
For attached PRs/MRs, describe remaining work on that candidate. For human-owned
work, name the action and completion evidence; an unresolved human decision may
be that outcome, while dependent implementation remains blocked.

## Keep readiness truthful

A draft may describe missing decisions or blocked work. Mark it ready only when
its recipient can act on the stated outcome without inventing a consequential
decision or obtaining an unresolved prerequisite. Establish required permissions
and actual availability of predecessor results; closure alone is insufficient.
Configured blocking relationships remain binding even if the brief reads well.

Otherwise retain a non-ready disposition and identify the missing decision,
blocker, or proof while continuing independent work. This brief creates no
implementation, human-decision, or tracker-mutation authority.
