# Recovery

Use for interrupted, blocked, or off-contract controlled assignments. Preserve
the candidate, accepted work, unfinished obligations, useful context, active
writers and subprocesses, and custody before choosing a response. Actor status
does not prove quiescence or release; never create a competing writer.

## Resolve the cause

- **Consequential question:** supply the decision or missing codebase knowledge,
  clarify the assignment, and return implementation to the worker.
- **Missing prerequisite or contradictory acceptance:** resolve the requirement,
  access, or environment problem rather than raising model effort.
- **Correctable implementation finding:** reuse a suitable worker with focused
  findings and freedom to choose the repair.
- **Changed reasoning need:** preserve evidence and re-scope through
  [Shared model routing](model-policy.md), retaining the controlled support limit.
  Large volume alone does not change the route.
- **Interruption or replacement:** establish quiescence, inspect preserved work,
  and transfer the assignment, evidence, limits, and exclusive custody. Resume
  useful context when safe.

Process repeated returns once and reuse still-valid evidence and prior acceptance.
Resume authorized work when the cause is resolved. If required input or authority
remains unavailable, preserve the work and report the specific incomplete outcome;
interruption does not reset accepted requirements.

Parallel assignments use [parallel recovery](../../parallel-implement/references/recovery.md)
for their lanes and replacements. Separately requested
[takeover](../../takeover/SKILL.md) owns adopting another PR, branch, or conversation,
including contributor credit; replacing a worker does not select that workflow.
