# Planned delivery

Use a checkpoint when a lead decision or interim review could prevent meaningful
rework. Choose the smallest useful handoff and reuse any existing plan.

The worker can adapt its implementation phases while preserving accepted product
stages. Keep routine milestones within worker custody. At a chosen checkpoint,
return a stable candidate, release custody, and provide the evidence needed for
the decision or review, including affected earlier integration. Assess the work
within the checkpoint's accepted scope.

Correct in-scope problems and continue the accepted plan without waiting for
another user instruction. When accepted meaning changes while assignments or
proof depend on it, use
[Active delivery revisions](../../shape-work/references/active-delivery-revisions.md)
before resuming affected work.

Review the complete accumulated candidate after the final return. Reuse earlier
evidence while its inputs remain valid; intermediate passes do not replace
whole-result acceptance or missing required proof.
