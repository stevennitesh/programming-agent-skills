# Planned delivery

Use when chosen phases or checkpoints help continuity or resolve uncertainty.
Choose their format and granularity; no plan artifact or fixed review schedule
is required.

Preserve accepted product stages and sequencing while adapting implementation
steps. Reuse existing plans. A phase can remain worker-owned end to end; a
checkpoint deliberately returns a stable candidate to the lead for a decision
or review. Phases do not automatically create checkpoints.

At a chosen checkpoint, establish its accepted behavior and deciding evidence,
release custody, and assess affected earlier integration. Unfinished future phases
are not checkpoint defects. Between checkpoints, implementation stays worker-owned
and the lead waits for meaningful events under the main workflow.

Correct in-scope problems and continue the accepted plan without waiting for
another user instruction. When accepted meaning changes while assignments or
proof depend on it, use
[Active delivery revisions](../../shape-work/references/active-delivery-revisions.md)
before resuming affected work.

Review the complete accumulated candidate after the final return. Reuse earlier
evidence while its inputs remain valid; intermediate passes do not replace
whole-result acceptance or missing required proof.
