# Historical evidence

Read when the question depends on what was available, known, published, or
effective at a cutoff.

Fix the cutoff and relevant availability channel. Distinguish the date of the
underlying event, publication/release time, earliest availability established
through the inspected channel, effective date, and later corrections or revisions.
A current page does not establish what it said earlier. Publication alone does
not establish that a particular decision-maker possessed the information.

Use the applicable archived version, release, revision, or data vintage and record
its identity. Keep later evidence separate from what could support a decision at
the cutoff. If the historical channel or vintage cannot be established, preserve
supported contemporary facts but leave the historical availability claim unknown.
