# Optional high-assurance review

Use only for an explicit user request for high assurance or multiple independent
reviewers. The top-level coordinator owns dispatch, convergence, and the final
decision. Reviewers do not delegate, repair, publish comments, or merge. This mode
adds independent scrutiny, not a different standard of truth.

## Fix the candidate and factual brief

Use the main skill's selected comparison and candidate. Provide both reviewers
the same immutable snapshot or exclusive read-only target, including surrounding
code, relevant configuration, and evidence needed beyond the diff. Worktrees alone
do not isolate shared services, databases, or mutable external proof.

Give the accepted request, governing sources, candidate identity, scope, relevant
handoffs, available proof, and material limits. For a completed ticket graph,
include its accepted commitments and actual dispositions; review the combined
outcome rather than treating individually completed tickets as integration proof.
Keep coordinator suspicions, desired verdict, and peer findings out of the initial
brief. A missing required source is a coverage gap, not a reason to invent intent.

## Two fresh reviewers

Dispatch two fresh-context read-only reviewers, separate from the implementation
and integration authors and from each other. Use host-supported tools and models;
do not prescribe provider names. Give necessary factual context explicitly and
record the actual independence achieved. Do not claim memory-free or isolated
execution merely because the conversation was not inherited.

Both inspect the whole selected change, with different primary emphasis:

1. **Behavior and integration:** accepted meaning, coverage, callers, composed
   results, migration, and complete replacement or removal.
2. **Engineering quality:** correctness, failure behavior, ownership, data shape,
   simplicity, resource consequences, and discriminating proof.

The emphasis does not exempt either reviewer from material obligations. Each
independently traces consequential handoffs to the real consumer and terminal
effect, including different request profiles or exhaustion outcomes when relevant.
Producer-only checks cannot establish that a consumer uses a fresh result rather
than ignoring it, using stale identity, or ending the transition early.

Ask each to return finding candidates, inspected coverage, and material gaps.
No finding quota and no duplicate test runs for symmetry. If two fresh reviewers
or the required snapshot cannot be obtained, report the high-assurance mode as
incomplete and return any useful ordinary-review evidence without mislabeling it.
Additional reviewers require the user's authorization; no recursive fanout.

## Converge under evidence

After independent reviews finish, inspect every proposed finding that could affect
the conclusion against the fixed candidate and contrary evidence. Deduplicate
overlaps and resolve disagreements through code, contracts, or proportionate
checks rather than votes. Agreement can guide attention; a lone finding may still
be decisive. Keep a short disposition for each material disputed or rejected claim.

Account for accepted commitments, affected entry paths and cross-ticket handoffs,
displaced behavior, applicable failure and compatibility cases, and required proof.
Two completed reviews do not close an uncovered obligation. A material unresolved
dispute remains a coverage or evidence gap.

Recheck the fixed identity and return a gate decision under Finding standards,
with findings, coverage limits, decisive checks, reviewer identities, and the
independence actually established. If the coordinator authored the implementation,
disclose that distinction rather than claiming the final synthesis is independent.
The two independent reviewer contributions remain separately attributable.

For a repaired successor, use fresh reviews of the repair and affected acceptance,
carrying prior finding IDs. Broaden when the repair changes scope materially.
Preserve earlier evidence only where it remains applicable to the new candidate.
